**Not legal advice:** this page sums up Colorado's dig law and some neighborhood review rules in plain English.

## What sits between the first call and a finished yard?

Five steps, and only the last one is the crew's. The rest run on other people's clocks.

| Step | Whose clock | What the rules say |
|---|---|---|
| Visit, measure, quote | The installer's booking calendar | Ask how far out they are scheduling this season |
| HOA or metro-district review | Your design committee | 14 to 60 days in the rules below |
| Town permit | Firestone today; possibly Greeley later | Firestone requires one before turf goes in |
| Colorado 811 locate | State law | Two business days' notice, not counting the day you file; 811 says to allow 3 full business days |
| Work on site | The yard, the ground and the weather | Size, access and soil set it; see below |

The order matters as much as the length. Locate marks expire, so the ticket goes in once the approvals are in hand and a start date is set, not while a committee is still deciding.

## How long does HOA or metro-district review take?

As long as your neighborhood's rules allow. Colorado law bars an HOA from prohibiting artificial turf in the backyard of a detached single-family home, but the association can still apply design rules and review the plans ([C.R.S. 38-33.3-106.5, from SB23-178](https://leg.colorado.gov/bill_files/92307/download)), so the review stays on the calendar. Five written rules from around Northern Colorado:

| Neighborhood (town) | What its rules say about timing |
|---|---|
| New Windsor ([Windsor](/areas/windsor-co/)) | A written response within 14 days on plans for changes to an existing home, with a $50 submittal fee ([design guidelines](https://www.newwindsormetro.org/files/72102649d/New+Windsor_Brunner+Farm+Design+Guidelines+-+5-16-24+-+Final.pdf)) |
| Severance Shores ([Severance](/areas/severance-co/)) | Landscaping plans approved before work starts; no answer in 30 days means you contact the district, and a second submittal left unanswered for 30 days counts as approved ([guidelines](https://ssmd.specialdistrict.org/files/3cc9774d3/SSMD+-+Amended+RIGSR+09.05.24.pdf)) |
| Barefoot Lakes ([Firestone](/areas/firestone-co/)) | The committee rules on a complete request within 60 days ([guidelines](https://www.stvrainmd.live/files/5457a4faf/SVLMD+-+2026+08+07+-+Amended+%26+Restated+Residential+Improvement+Guidelines+%26+Site+Restrictions+for+Barefoot.pdf)) |
| Timnath Ranch ([Timnath](/areas/timnath-co/)) | A $150 review fee is due with the application, and nothing is reviewed until it is complete and paid ([district page](https://www.timnathranchmd.live/architectural-review)) |
| Settlers Village ([Milliken](/areas/milliken-co/)) | The application commits the owner to no work until written approval arrives ([application](https://www.settlersvillagehoa.net/_files/ugd/98f97b_4a782ecf39ca48828a67b995270958bf.pdf)) |

Where a yard lands in that range comes down to two things: whether the packet is complete the first time, and when the committee next meets. Several of these clocks start only at a complete submittal, so a missing drawing can cost a full cycle. What goes in the packet is in our [guide to HOA approval for artificial turf](/guides/hoa-turf-approval/). Ask the management company for the submittal deadline ahead of the next meeting before you pick a start date.

## Does my town add a permit?

**Firestone: yes.** The development code requires a town permit before artificial turf is installed, with no fee for single-family homes, and the Town allows turf in the rear and side yards of single-family homes ([Firestone Development Code 16.6.4](https://library.municode.com/co/firestone/codes/municipal_code?nodeId=TIT16FIDECO_CH6DEDEST_16.6.4LASCFE); [Town of Firestone](https://firestoneco.gov/175/Development-Code)). The code doesn't say how long review takes, so ask Firestone Planning before you set a start date.

**Greeley: possibly later.** The city is drafting a code change that would allow front-yard turf on houses with a permit; as of September 24, 2026, no ordinance had been adopted ([council status report](https://greeleyco.api.civicclerk.com/v1/Meetings/GetMeetingFileStream(fileId=9580,plainText=false))). If it passes, a front-yard job in [Greeley](/areas/greeley-co/) gains that permit step.

**Elsewhere:** the town codes in our [guide to turf rules in Northern Colorado](/guides/turf-rules-northern-colorado/) set no turf permit for an existing home that we found. Codes change, though, and a metro district's rules sit on top of the town's. It depends on the address, so ask your town's Planning department when in doubt.

## When does the Colorado 811 locate fit in?

After the approvals, a few business days before the dig. Colorado law bars anyone from starting an excavation without first notifying Colorado 811, at least two business days ahead, not counting the day of notice ([C.R.S. 9-1.5-103](https://ops.colorado.gov/sites/ops/files/documents/UDPSafetyCommissionStatutes010623.pdf)). Colorado 811 tells homeowners to wait at least 3 full business days and to track the request online ([Colorado 811](https://www.colorado811.org/residential-digging)).

Three details move a start date:

- **The clock starts at the confirmation.** A request isn't valid until its email confirmation arrives, utilities answer within two business days after that, and a request received after 2 p.m. Mountain Time may be processed the next business day ([Colorado 811, residential requests](https://www.colorado811.org/residential-digging/residential-requests)).
- **Marks expire.** Under the same statute they count for no more than 30 calendar days after the locate's due date. If the dig runs longer, the excavator has to notify 811 again at least two business days before that period ends. A ticket filed the week the HOA packet goes in can lapse before a 60-day review is over.
- **Public lines only.** Colorado 811's locates leave out privately owned lines such as sprinkler lines. Those, and the rest of the homeowner's list, are in [what to do before the turf crew arrives](/guides/preparing-for-turf-installation-day/).

## How many days is the crew on site?

It depends on the yard. Here is what moves the number of days on any job:

- **Size and shape.** One open rectangle goes faster than the same area broken up by beds, curves and trees.
- **The way in.** A machine that fits through the gate moves soil and rock far faster than wheelbarrows through a narrow side gate.
- **What comes out.** Sod and roots, old rock or mulch, buried landscape fabric, and every load that has to be hauled away.
- **The ground.** Clay that needs more digging or a drainage fix.
- **Sprinklers.** Capping heads under the new turf and rerouting lines that still water trees and beds.
- **Features.** A [putting green](/services/putting-greens/) needs shaping and cups; a [dog area](/services/pet-turf/) may need drainage work and a rinse spot.
- **What the dig turns up.** Buried concrete, rubble or an unmarked line can stop work until it's sorted out.

Ask each installer how many crew days they expect for your yard and what would add one. A good answer names a number and your yard's own risks, such as the gate width, the clay or a tree's roots, and says what happens to the schedule if it rains mid-job. More questions are in [what to ask a turf installer](/guides/questions-to-ask-turf-installer/), and the [installation page](/services/artificial-turf-installation/) walks through the work itself.

## What can stretch the schedule once work starts?

Weather, mostly. On clay the delay outlasts the storm.

- **Wet clay.** Longmont tells residents its clay soils absorb water slowly ([City of Longmont](https://longmontcolorado.gov/water/water-conservation/conserving-water-outdoors/)), so ground like that stays wet well after a soaking. Colorado's highway specifications show how much moisture matters to compaction: most fill may not go into standing water, and aggregate base must be compacted within 2 percent of its optimum moisture ([CDOT Standard Specifications](https://www.codot.gov/business/designsupport/construction-specifications/2025-construction-specifications/specs-book/2025-cdot-specs-book.pdf)). A turf base is compacted crushed rock on a smaller scale, so base work waits for the ground to dry.
- **Spring rain.** At the Fort Collins NOAA station, May is the wettest month, with 2.72 inches over 5.8 days of at least 0.10 inch, and April averages 2.10 inches ([NOAA climate normals](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&stations=USC00053005&format=json&dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL,MLY-PRCP-AVGNDS-GE010HI,MLY-TMIN-AVGNDS-LSTH032)). A wet week in either month can push a start date back.
- **Frozen ground.** The same highway specifications bar frozen material from fill. At the Fort Collins station, lows reach 32°F or colder on 21.5 to 30 nights a month from November through March. Whether a winter install makes sense is in [when to install artificial turf](/guides/when-to-install-artificial-turf/).
- **Cold seams.** Seam adhesives have temperature limits. MAPEI's data sheet for one turf seam adhesive says not to use it below 5°C (41°F) ([MAPEI](https://cdnmedia.mapei.com/docs/librariesprovider2/products-documents/1_00260_ultrabond-turf-pu-2k_en_093f204613974a27aeab019c84085115.pdf?sfvrsn=1831d6bc_0)). Other products state other limits, so a cold snap can move the seaming day.
- **A soaked lawn at the start.** SYNLawn's installation guidelines say to have the site's water turned off a few days before installation ([SYNLawn](https://pdf.lowes.com/productdocuments/240a8581-4267-4cdf-aa5f-d4d1e250fec6/44269346.pdf)). Watering right up to the dig puts the crew on wet ground from the first cut.

## When can we use the yard?

Once the turf is down, seamed, edged, infilled and brushed, you can walk on it. The seams are the part to go easy on at first. SYNLawn's guidelines say a seam needs the drying or curing time its adhesive maker recommends, and that weight such as sand bags on the seams helps the bond; MAPEI's sheet gives its adhesive 24 hours to harden at ambient temperature.

Ask the installer which adhesive they use, whether weights stay on overnight, and how long to keep dogs, a party or a play set off the seam lines.
