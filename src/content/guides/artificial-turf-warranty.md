  question: "What does a \"limited warranty\" on artificial turf actually cover, and what does it leave out?"
  answer: "The yarn and backing, not the labor. A turf maker's terms cover UV breakdown, usually measured as lost strength rather than color, on a sliding scale that shrinks with age. faq:
  - q: "Do I have to register the turf for the maker's terms to apply?"
    a: "Often, yes. SYNLawn's terms, which it titles limited, make timely registration a condition of coverage, and Purchase Green's must be registered within 60 days of purchase. Federal rules treat a registration-card condition as an unreasonable duty under a \"full warranty\", but not under a limited one. Ask who registers the product, and keep the confirmation."
  - q: "If the maker replaces a section, does the clock restart?"
    a: "Not under the terms we read. SYNLawn and Purchase Green both give repaired or replaced turf only the balance of the original term, so a patch laid in year 6 of a 10-year scale has 4 years left."
  - q: "Does a turf maker pay cash on a valid claim?"
    a: "Rarely. Smart Turf, TigerTurf and EnvyLawn issue credit good only toward their own turf, with no cash payment. SYNLawn switches from repair to credit toward a new project once its terms are over 6 years old, and that credit has to be used within 18 months."
  - q: "Is a \"lifetime\" term better than a 15-year one?"
    a: "Read the scale, not the label. The FTC's business guide notes that lifetime can be used in at least three ways, and its advertising guides say an ad should make clear whose life it means. Purchase Green's residential Limited Lifetime scale pays 25% in years 13 to 15 and 15% from year 16 onward."
  - q: "Who do I contact first when something goes wrong?"
    a: "Usually the installer. SYNLawn's terms send claims to the installer the turf was bought from, within 30 days of finding the defect, and TigerTurf and EnvyLawn ask for a written description with photos as soon as the problem shows. Date the photos, and keep the invoice that names the product."

The makers' wording below is quoted from their published terms on September 25, 2026. Makers revise them, and the version that binds you is the one attached to your contract.

## Who stands behind the turf: the maker or the installer?

Both, for different failures.

| | Maker's product terms | Installer's workmanship terms |
|---|---|---|
| Written by | The turf manufacturer | The company that lays the turf |
| Covers | The yarn and backing: UV breakdown, tuft bind, backing | The job: seams, edges, fastening, the base and its drainage |
| Labor to fix it | Left out, in the terms we read | The reason the document exists |
| Length | Long, on a sliding scale | Short: SYNLawn recommends 3 years, and ForeverLawn says its dealers give at least two |

SYNLawn is "responsible for product only, not the installation labor required in conjunction with turf to remedy defect" ([SYNLawn](https://lowes.synlawn.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/SY24247_Lowes_ProductWarranty_v1.pdf)), and EnvyLawn leaves out "the cost of tear out and installation" ([EnvyLawn](https://envylawn.com/resources/warranty-landscape-15-year/)).

So a seam that opens or a base that settles in year two is workmanship, and the maker's paperwork doesn't come into it. When the yarn breaks down in year nine, the maker may send turf, and someone still pays to cut out the old and lay the new. Base failures are excluded by name: SYNLawn's terms exclude "improper subsurface preparation or drainage," and Turf Distributors' "incorrect base preparation, seam failure, or installation errors" ([Turf Distributors](https://www.turfdistributors.com/warranty-policy/)). On [Northern Colorado clay](/guides/artificial-turf-on-clay-soil/), [settling](/guides/artificial-turf-sinking-low-spots/), [seams](/guides/visible-seams-in-artificial-turf/) and [lifting edges](/guides/turf-edges-lifting/) fall to the installer's terms or to you.

Ask for a workmanship term that outlasts at least one winter: NOAA's Fort Collins station averages 150 days a year with a low at or below 32°F ([NOAA](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-annualseasonal-1991-2020&stations=USC00053005&format=json)); a base that hasn't met a thaw hasn't been tested.

## What does a fade or UV clause actually promise?

Less than the word "fade" suggests. Most terms we read define UV failure by strength lost, not color, and pay a shrinking share each year:

| Maker's terms | Counts as UV failure | Share covered |
|---|---|---|
| [SYNLawn](https://lowes.synlawn.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/SY24247_Lowes_ProductWarranty_v1.pdf) (Lowe's channel, 2024) | Over 40% of face-yarn weight or of yarn strength lost | 100% in years 1–3, 50% in years 4–5, 25% in years 6–7, 15% in years 8–10 |
| [PreGra](https://www.pregra.com/warranty/) | Color fades over 15%, or pile height drops over 50% | 100% through year 4, then 70, 50, 30, 25, 25 and 20% in years 5–10 |
| [TigerTurf](https://www.tigerturf.com/us/warranty/) | Tensile strength down over 50% | 100% in years 1–8, 50% in years 9–12, 25% in years 13–15 |
| [EnvyLawn](https://envylawn.com/resources/warranty-landscape-15-year/) (landscape) | Tensile strength down over 50% | 100% in years 1–10, 50% in years 11–12, 25% in years 13–15 |
| [Smart Turf](https://smartturf.com/warranty) | Over 50% of tensile strength lost | Falls 6.67% a year, to 0% at the end of year 15 |

What the table hides:

- **Color alone rarely qualifies.** SYNLawn calls slight color variation over time "normative" and won't cover it unless it is fiber degradation. Only PreGra puts a number on color. How altitude sun wears yarn is in [does artificial turf fade](/guides/does-artificial-turf-fade/).
- **The headline number is usually the UV term.** Smart Turf's 15 years cover UV; manufacturing defects get "a period of one year" ([Smart Turf](https://smartturf.com/warranty)).
- **The clock can start at the factory.** TigerTurf and EnvyLawn count 15 years "from the date of manufacture," so a roll that sat in a warehouse arrives with some of its term used.
- **A putting green can carry a shorter term.** EnvyLawn's putting-green surface gets 8 years from manufacture, against 15 for its landscape turf, and its terms exclude "metal cleats" and "any issues stemming from the installation" ([EnvyLawn putting green](https://envylawn.com/resources/warranty-putting-green/)). For a [backyard putting green](/services/putting-greens/), ask for the green's own document.
- **A sliding scale is a trade.** The Synthetic Turf Council notes that terms without one "are usually written for a shorter period of time, but cover the full cost of repair or replacement" ([Synthetic Turf Council](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.syntheticturfcouncil.org/resource/resmgr/guidelines/STC_Considerations_When_Buyi.pdf)).

## Which exclusions matter most in Northern Colorado?

| What happens here | What the terms say | More |
|---|---|---|
| Reflected sun from Low-E windows melts a streak | Excluded by SYNLawn, PreGra, Smart Turf, TigerTurf, EnvyLawn, Purchase Green and Turf Distributors | [melting near windows](/guides/artificial-turf-melting-from-windows/) |
| Hail: 536 NOAA reports in Weld County and 253 in Larimer, 2011 through 2025 ([NOAA NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/swdi/stormevents/csvfiles/)) | None names hail; most exclude "Acts of God" or "acts of nature" | [hail and insurance](/guides/does-hail-damage-artificial-turf/) |
| A fire pit, grill or outdoor kitchen next to the turf | TigerTurf, EnvyLawn and Purchase Green exclude fire; Turf Distributors excludes high heat from "fire pits" | [fire pits on turf](/guides/fire-pit-on-artificial-turf/) |
| Clearing snow | None names shovels or snowblowers; Purchase Green excludes "machinery", Turf Distributors "sharp objects" and "excessive weight on turf" | [snow removal](/guides/artificial-turf-snow-removal/) |
| Dogs | Purchase Green excludes damage by "animals"; Turf Distributors treats "excessive pet waste buildup" as improper maintenance | [dog urine smell](/guides/dog-urine-smell-artificial-turf/) |
| Cleaners and odor products | SYNLawn excludes halogen chemicals "such as chlorine" and applications "with a PH greater than five (5)"; Purchase Green, a pH below 5 or above 9 | [cleaning turf](/guides/how-to-clean-artificial-turf/) |

NOAA's Fort Collins station averages 51.4 inches of snow a year, so ask before the first storm which tools your maker allows, and check any cleaner's pH against your terms before it goes on ([Purchase Green](https://files.purchasegreen.com/docs/Artificial_Grass_Lifetime_Warranty.pdf)).

## Does a town, HOA or metro district here set its own terms?

Three that we found do:

- **[Firestone](/areas/firestone-co/).** The town's code requires turf materials to be "protected with a minimum of an eight (8) year warranty" ([Firestone Development Code 16.6.4](https://library.municode.com/co/firestone/codes/municipal_code?nodeId=TIT16FIDECO_CH6DEDEST_16.6.4LASCFE)). Not legal advice: whether a scale paying 15% by year 8 meets it depends on how the town reads a pro-rated term, so ask Firestone Planning before you order. Its full turf standard is in the [spec sheet guide](/guides/turf-spec-sheet-for-colorado/).
- **Barefoot Lakes, in Firestone.** The St. Vrain Lakes Metropolitan Districts can require turf to be replaced once its life expectancy "has been reached, typically 15-20 years" ([St. Vrain Lakes Metropolitan Districts](https://www.stvrainmd.live/files/5457a4faf/SVLMD+-+2026+08+07+-+Amended+%26+Restated+Residential+Improvement+Guidelines+%26+Site+Restrictions+for+Barefoot.pdf)). By year 15, none of the five scales above pays more than 25%, so replacing it then is mostly on you. The same guidelines make owners "responsible for being aware of the effect of any Improvement on any applicable builder warranties." On a [new build](/guides/new-build-backyard-turf/), ask the builder in writing before anyone regrades or trenches near the house.
- **New Windsor Metropolitan District, in [Windsor](/areas/windsor-co/).** Its design review committee considers backyard turf only if it is "warranted for a period of 10 years against discoloration and wear" and professionally installed, and it wants a copy of the product's terms with the application ([New Windsor Metropolitan District](https://www.newwindsormetro.org/files/72102649d/New+Windsor_Brunner+Farm+Design+Guidelines+-+5-16-24+-+Final.pdf)). Since most makers measure UV failure by strength, ask the committee whether a strength-based clause counts against discoloration. It can also make you remove or replace turf it finds deteriorated, whatever the maker pays by then.

Other HOAs and metro districts set their own lists: see the [HOA approval guide](/guides/hoa-turf-approval/) and [turf on a custom home](/guides/custom-home-landscape-turf/).

## Can I read the terms before I sign?

Yes. Not legal advice: this is a plain-English summary of federal rules.

- **Before the sale.** The FTC's Pre-Sale Availability Rule requires the seller of a product costing more than $15 that comes with written terms to make the text available for you to examine before you buy ([16 CFR 702.3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-702/section-702.3); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/magnuson-moss-warranty-act-pre-sale-availability-written-warranty-terms)). Turf counts: the rule covers property "intended to be attached to or installed in any real property" ([16 CFR 702.1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-702/section-702.1)).
- **At your kitchen table.** Buy at home, even from a rep you invited in, and before the sale the rep must say, aloud and in any written materials shown, that copies are there to inspect.
- **What the document must say.** Whether coverage is limited to the original purchaser, and when the term starts if not on the purchase date ([16 CFR 701.3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-701/section-701.3)). It must be titled full or limited ([16 CFR 700.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-700/section-700.6)); a full one on a product that works only once installed must include the installation free ([16 CFR 700.9](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-700/section-700.9)). The FTC's own example calls coverage limited because the customer must "pay labor" ([FTC business guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law)), so on a limited document, expect the labor to be yours.

## Does the coverage go with the house when I sell?

Usually not the maker's. TigerTurf, EnvyLawn and Smart Turf cover only the original purchaser, and PreGra's needs its written consent to transfer. SYNLawn's transfers when the property changes hands and SYNLawn is notified; Purchase Green's, with its transfer certificate. Under federal rules, a full one can't cut off a later owner during its term; a limited one can, and has to say so.

When you're [selling a house with turf](/guides/does-artificial-turf-increase-home-value/), hand the buyer a folder: the maker's terms and registration confirmation, the invoice naming the product, the install date, and the installer's terms, with whether they transfer. Buying one, ask the seller for the same folder.

## What should I get in writing before I sign?

1. Both documents, the maker's and the installer's, naming the same product as the spec sheet.
2. What counts as UV failure, whether color alone ever qualifies, and the share covered each year.
3. Whether a claim pays in repair, replacement or credit, and who pays the labor.
4. When the clock starts: installation, purchase or manufacture.
5. Where a dispute is heard: SYNLawn names Georgia courts; Smart Turf, arbitration in Los Angeles County.
6. The workmanship term's length, what it covers (seams, edges, settling, puddling), and whether it transfers.
7. On a project with other trades (a patio, outdoor kitchen, lighting or pool), whose terms cover each edge where the turf meets their work.

Then check the company. The Synthetic Turf Council says coverage is "only as good as the company that stands behind it when a warranty claim is submitted, perhaps years after the synthetic grass is installed." More in [questions to ask a turf installer](/guides/questions-to-ask-turf-installer/), [checking the base went in](/guides/check-turf-base-was-installed/) and our [installation page](/services/artificial-turf-installation/).
